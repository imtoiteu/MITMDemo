🔐 Key Concepts

  • Session Hijacking: If an attacker captures your session cookie over HTTP, they can impersonate you without knowing your password.
  • Message Interception: Every chat message is a separate HTTP request — all readable.
  • TLS Protection: HTTPS wraps the entire HTTP conversation in encryption — sender, content, and cookies are all hidden.
