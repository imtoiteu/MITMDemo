⚠️ Real-World Risk

  • An attacker on the same coffee-shop WiFi can capture the exact transfer amount and recipient account.
  • They can modify the POST body (MITM) to change the recipient before the server processes it.
  • HTTPS prevents both eavesdropping and modification — the browser and server verify the TLS certificate.
  • Always look for the 🔒 padlock in your browser before entering financial information.
